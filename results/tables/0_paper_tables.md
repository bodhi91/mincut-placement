* Metric: HPWL after detailed placement, before DPO (`hpwl_dpl`), mean over seeds per floorplan, ratio to RePlAce on the same floorplan, geometric mean over floorplans; (wins/n) = floorplans where the placer beats RePlAce / floorplans compared.
* A RePlAce run fails if it diverges, does not legalize, or stops above its 20 % overflow target (`converged` = 0 in `replace_convergence.csv`). An RB run fails if its partitioner returned no partition at the root bisection or on more than 1 % of all bisections (`verdict` = `fail` in `rb_engine_reports.csv`), or if it produced no legal placement.
* Every ratio is over the floorplans *common* to the configurations compared (none of them fails on it).
