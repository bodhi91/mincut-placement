# 7. Global routing on the few-row set

Source: E17 (`e17_krow`, RePlAce / Capo / RB with TP, HM, MK, FP) and X3 (`x3_mega`, RB+k (TP)), the same runs as tables 1 and 4, one seed. After `detailed_placement` and DPO every run was global-routed with `global_route -allow_congestion -congestion_iterations 30` on the platform's signal layers (Nangate45 metal2--metal10 with a 0.5 layer adjustment, ASAP7 M2--M6 with 0.25). RWL = FastRoute's total wirelength (GRT-0018); overflow = the `Total` line of the final congestion report (GRT-0096): total overflow and the maximum per-edge overflow in the horizontal and vertical direction. HPWL = after detailed placement, before DPO (the metric of tables 1--6). Ratios are placer / RePlAce on the same floorplan, geomean over floorplans, (wins / n).

RePlAce failure rule: a run that diverged, did not legalize or stopped above its 0.2 density-overflow target (`results/x3_mega/replace_convergence.csv`, parsed from the RePlAce logs) is excluded from every ratio; 137 of the 180 floorplans remain. The *common set* is the 87 floorplans on which, in addition, every placer's placement was routed.

Unit note: the ASAP7 checkpoints of aes, gcd, jpeg and swerv carry DEF units 100 with 1000 dbu/um, and GRT-0018 divides by the DEF units, so their printed wirelength is 10x too large; the absolute values below multiply it by 0.1. Ratios between placers and all overflow figures are unaffected.

Regenerate: `python3 exp/grt_tables.py`.
